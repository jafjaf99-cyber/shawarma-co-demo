Our story

A father, a son and a saj.

Shawarma Co is a family business, started by a father and son. It began as a single brick-and-mortar shop in Garden City, in the same building where the very first Little Caesars opened its doors in 1959.

More than six years later, we’re making shawarma on the saj in four cities across Metro Detroit.

  1. Garden CityWhere it began. A small counter and a hot saj.
  2. LivoniaThe same wraps, bowls and garlic sauce, a few miles up the road.
  3. DearbornShawarma in a city that knows its shawarma.
  4. Sterling HeightsNewest kitchen, now serving the east side.
